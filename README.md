# Warriors-battle
**Hollow Knight Duel** is a 2D side-view boss-fight game about timing and nerve. You face Ser Varkus, a towering armored knight twice your size, in a dark torch-lit arena. Parry his white-glow attacks to break his posture and open him up, and roll or jump through the red ones that can't be blocked. The fight has three phases: the knight hits hard and slow, then fast and evasive, and finally collapses so his spirit can rise from the armor and fight with flying strikes, spectral swords, and a ghost-horse stampede. Move with A/D, jump with W, attack with Space, roll with Q, and parry with F.
