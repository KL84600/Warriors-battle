Parry (F, even in the air) a glowing fireball or white-glowing blade to counter: white glow = parry it, red glow = dodge it (roll away or jump). Lightning: roll out of the red marks on the ground. A good parry resets your parry cooldown. Press 2 for Ser Varkus or 3 for the Thunder King on the title screen. Keyboard required.
